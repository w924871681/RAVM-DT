# Data attribution and licence notes: E4

This release contains processed inputs derived from the following datasets and locally trained model checkpoints. The original data archives are not included. The processed inputs are modified forms of the original tabular data: the scripts select columns, normalize values, construct time-series features, and create task-specific arrays. The corresponding `.npz` metadata and group configurations identify the original file hashes and transformations.

## UCI Appliances Energy Prediction

Luis Candanedo (2017), *Appliances Energy Prediction*, UCI Machine Learning Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VC8G. Dataset page: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/374/appliances+energy+prediction. The provider identifies the dataset licence as [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The UCI T1–T3 processed input arrays are adaptations and retain this attribution. Obtain the original `energydata_complete.csv` from UCI.

## ThermBuild

Matthias Kersken, Fabian Raisch, Markus Male and Benjamin Tischler (2026), *ThermBuild: Real-world and simulated thermal data from 960 residential multi-zone buildings in Central Europe*, Fraunhofer Fordatis, https://doi.org/10.24406/fordatis/445. The provider's full metadata record is https://fordatis.fraunhofer.de/handle/fordatis/486?locale=en&mode=full and identifies [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The two ThermBuild processed input arrays are adaptations and are made available under CC BY-SA 4.0; retain attribution, the licence, and the indication of changes. Obtain the original measurement archives directly from Fordatis.

These notices apply to the respective dataset-derived portions and do not relicense other third-party material. Model checkpoints in this release were generated by the authors from the identified processed inputs; they are not asserted to be provider-distributed models. The presence of files and licences does not establish a full clean-environment rerun. Alibaba E3 inputs and weights are excluded from this release.
